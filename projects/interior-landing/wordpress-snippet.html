FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does interior design cost?

It depends on size, scope and materials. Our packages start from ₹1,100/sq ft, and you get an itemised quote after the site visit.

How long does a project take?

A typical 2–3 BHK takes 10–12 weeks from sign-off. Offices and shops vary with scope.

Do you take both design-only and full turnkey projects?

Both. Most clients choose turnkey so one team is accountable from design to handover.

What are your payment terms?

Milestone-based: booking, design approval, material delivery, execution stages and handover.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes, 1 to 10 years depending on the package, covering workmanship and materials.
