Questions?

Aapke sawaal, humare jawab

Kya yeh bachchon aur senior citizens ke liye safe hai?

Yes. Hum slow-drive option dete hain. Pregnant women, back/neck problems wale guests aur 3 se kam age ke bachche dune bashing skip karke camp enjoy kar sakte hain.

Kya veg / Jain khana milega?

Haan, BBQ buffet me veg aur Jain options available hain. Booking ke time bata dein.

Kya hotel se pickup free hai?

Haan, Dubai ke zyadatar areas se free pickup & drop included hai. Sharjah / Abu Dhabi ke liye thoda extra lag sakta hai.

Kitna time lagta hai aur kya pehnu?

Total 6–7 hours. Light cotton kapde aur sports shoes/sandals pehnein. Sunglasses aur dupatta/scarf dust se bachne ke liye rakhein.
