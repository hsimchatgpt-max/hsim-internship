Part 3

Kaun sa platform, kaise theek karna hai

Har platform par click karke steps dekho. Har ek mein login link aur kaun se documents lagenge, wo bhi likha hai. Hamesha apne hi login se kaam karna.

Pehle ye documents ek folder mein rakh lo

Signboard ke saath office ki photos · logo · rent agreement ya bijli bill (address proof) · ID proof · Section 8 certificate · wo mobile aur email jinse purane accounts bane the (OTP aayega).

Claim kaise karte hain (har platform ka common tareeka)
  1. Pehle search karo. Platform par "HSIM Karnal" ya "Hindustan School of Internet Marketing" search karo. Listing pehle se hai to nayi mat banao, nahi to duplicate ban jayegi.
  2. Claim button dhundo. Listing page par "Claim this business", "Is this your business?", "Own this business?" ya "Edit listing" likha hota hai.
  3. Login ya account banao. Company email (support@hsim.in) ya wo email jisse pehle bana tha. Personal email se mat banao, nahi to baad mein access kho jata hai.
  4. Verification. Phone OTP, call, email link ya document maangte hain. Verification ke time 90530 70530 wala phone paas rakho.
  5. NAP bharo. Verify hone ke baad Master NAP copy-paste karo, phir logo, photos, description aur website link daalo.
  6. Claim ka button na ho to site ke support ko mail ya contact form bhejo. Template: "Hello, I am the owner of HSIM - Hindustan School of Internet Marketing, Karnal. Please let me claim and update this listing. Correct details: SCO-112, 2nd Floor, Old Tehsil Market, GT Road, Karnal 132001, +91 90530 70530, support@hsim.in, Mon-Sat 9:30 AM - 5:30 PM. Listing link: (paste link)."
  7. Tracker mein likho. Listing ka link, kaun si email ya number se login kiya, aur date. Excel ka "Login Tracker" sheet isi ke liye hai.

Part 5

Uske baad: Chandigarh branch

Karnal poora saaf hone ke baad Chandigarh shuru hoga, taaki Google ko brand ek hi dikhe. Chandigarh ka NAP Karnal se alag rahega.

Chandigarh ke liye chahiye: pakka address aur floor, alag phone number, rent agreement ya bijli bill, signboard ke saath photos, aur naam jaise HSIM - Hindustan School of Internet Marketing, Chandigarh.

Alag Google Business Profile banegi, website par alag Chandigarh page, aur har directory mein alag listing. Karnal wali listing ko edit karke Chandigarh mat banana.

Chandigarh ke steps (Karnal poora hone ke baad)
  1. Sir se Chandigarh ka address, floor, phone, email aur opening date lo (Part 6 ke sawal 27 se 29).
  2. Chandigarh ki alag Master NAP row banao. Karnal ki row mat chhedna.
  3. Documents: address proof (rent agreement ya bijli bill), signboard ke saath photos, logo.
  4. Signboard lagne ka wait karo. Google verification ke liye signboard dikhna zaroori hai.
  5. Google Business Profile: business.google.com > Add business > naam "HSIM - Hindustan School of Internet Marketing, Chandigarh" > category > address aur map pin > phone > website (Chandigarh wala page).
  6. Verification video branch par hi banao (signboard, andar ka kaam).
  7. Website par alag Chandigarh page banao: address, phone, map, courses.
  8. Bing Places, Apple Business Connect aur Justdial par Chandigarh ki nayi listing banao.
  9. Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram mein "Locations" ya "Branches" mein Chandigarh jodo.
  10. Tracker mein Chandigarh ki alag listing, login aur status likho.
