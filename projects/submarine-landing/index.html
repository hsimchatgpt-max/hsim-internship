Got questions?

Everything you need to know

Is it safe for children and elderly guests?

Yes. The submarine operates at normal cabin pressure with certified crew and triple safety systems. Children 4+ and guests of any age can join; no medical fitness required for the standard tour.

I can’t swim / I’m claustrophobic. Can I still go?

Absolutely. You stay dry inside a spacious, bright cabin with big windows. Many claustrophobic guests are surprised how open it feels.

What if the weather is bad?

Safety first — we reschedule you for free or refund 100%, your choice.

Is the party suitable for kids?

The afternoon deck is family-friendly. Alcohol is served to 18+ only, and the DJ night after 7 PM is best for adults.

How long does it take and what should I bring?

About 2 hours in total (≈45 min underwater). Bring sunscreen, a camera and light clothing. Everything else is provided.

How do I get my ticket?

Instantly by email after booking. Show the QR code on your phone at the marina.
