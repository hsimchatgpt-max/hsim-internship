## 0. Decisions needed before any ad goes live (Day 1-3 gate)

| # | Decision | Why it blocks launch |
|---|----------|----------------------|
| 1 | Written terms: ranking rule, tie-break, assessment format, who evaluates, appeal window | "Transparent rankings" must be published, not promised |
| 2 | Refund rule (exact wording, see 3.6) | Ambiguous guarantees create disputes and breach ad-claim norms |
| 3 | Tax treatment of the ₹999 fee and course fee (GST inclusive/exclusive) | Must show the final payable amount; confirm with the CA |
| 4 | Enrollment deadline for award holders, and whether awards are transferable/combinable | Prevents "I won but it expired" complaints |
| 5 | Legal read of the offer | Paid entry + ranked prizes can be treated as a prize competition; the "scholarship" must be tied to skill-based assessment, not chance. Get a lawyer's view [T] |
| 6 | Venue, date, capacity, trainer names | Landing page and ads depend on them |
| 7 | One legal brand name | Public sources use both "Hindustan School of Internet Marketing" and "Haryana School of Internet Marketing" [V]. Pick the registered one |
| 8 | Capacity check | 300 award positions need at least ~300 assessed attendees to make sense. If venue/capacity is smaller, reduce tiers or run two sessions/hybrid |

**Offer-structure risk to flag:** 300 award positions out of N attendees means awards are very likely for most attendees if N is under ~400. This is generous (a plus for conversion) but the 10-20% discounts only cost money when someone enrols, so the real exposure is the 3 free seats (₹1,44,000 list value) and margin dilution. Model this in section 9.

## 5. Paid acquisition plan

### 5.1 Principles
Test small, learn fast, scale only on registrations that become attendees. Start every ad set with the same offer and one landing page, then test one variable at a time. Do not trust platform "leads"; trust confirmed paid registrations in your own sheet/CRM.

### 5.2 Meta Ads (primary volume driver)

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Objective | Sales/Conversions optimised on a web "Registration complete" event once ≥ ~30-50 events exist [A: platform learning thresholds]; before that, optimise for landing-page views/"Lead" event and test. Lead-form ads as an alternative test only if you can still collect consent and verify phone numbers |
| Audience hypotheses | H1 broad: 18-35, Chandigarh + ~25-30 km (Mohali, Panchkula, Zirakpur) with Advantage+ audience; H2 interest/behaviour layers (digital marketing, entrepreneurship, college); H3 geo expansion (Ambala, Patiala, Kharar); H4 owner segment: 25-50, small business/page admins; H5 lookalike from the registrant list once ≥ 100 consented contacts exist |
| Placements | Advantage+ placements first, then inspect IG Reels/Stories vs FB feed results |
| Creative variations (3 hooks × 2 formats) | Hook A career fear/clarity; Hook B local business proof; Hook C transparent awards. Formats: founder talking-head Reel, screen-record AI demo, static award table |
| Landing tests | LP-A hero outcome-led; LP-B hero offer-led (awards above the fold); test one change at a time |
| Retargeting | Video viewers (50%+), IG/FB engagers, landing-page visitors not registered (7 and 14 days), started-but-didn't-complete checkout. Eligibility: Pixel/CAPI events and consent per Meta policy, no sensitive-attribute targeting |
| Policy | Education is not a special ad category by default but claims must be accurate; no guaranteed-income/placement language; no "before/after" body-image style claims; disclosure of ₹999 and awards in the creative |

### 5.3 Google Ads

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Campaign 1: Search - workshop | Phrase/exact: "digital marketing workshop Chandigarh", "SEO workshop Chandigarh", "AI marketing workshop"; RSA with 12+ headlines; location Chandigarh tricity |
| Campaign 2: Search - course intent | "digital marketing course Chandigarh/Mohali/Panchkula" → workshop page as the offer (low-risk try-before-you-buy); negative keywords: free, jobs, salary, government, PDF, notes, online-only [adjust after search-terms report] |
| Campaign 3: Brand | "HSIM", "HSIM Karnal" to defend brand terms (cheap) |
| Smart bidding | Start with Maximise Clicks or manual CPC with cap for 3-5 days; switch to Maximise Conversions (registration) after ≥ ~15-30 conversions [A] |
| Assets | Sitelinks (terms, FAQ, venue), callouts (₹999, merit awards), structured snippets |
| Extras | Performance Max/Demand Gen: skip in the 20-day window; too little data |
| Policy | Education/competition wording is fine but no unsubstantiated "best" or guaranteed results; follow Google's misrepresentation policy |

### 5.4 Registration tracking
- Pixel + Conversions API / GA4 + Google Ads conversion tied to **payment success** (server-side where possible), not form-fills. Pass `value=999` and an order ID for de-duplication.
- UTMs on every link: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` (creative ID).
- Consent banner and privacy notice before tracking cookies fire (India's DPDP Act, 2023 and its rules; confirm current status with counsel).

### 5.5 Lead qualification questions (form step 2, optional where possible)
1. I am a: student / working professional / business owner / other.
2. Your main goal: get a job / freelance / grow my own business / just exploring.
3. Experience with digital marketing: none / some / working in it.
4. If you receive an award, would you be able to enrol by [deadline]? Yes / maybe / no.
5. Course fee budget comfort band (optional).
6. Preferred contact: WhatsApp / call / email, and consent checkboxes (separate, unticked).

Score: goal + timing + seriousness (A/B/C). Do not exclude paying registrants; use scores only to prioritise counselling calls.

### 5.6 Rules (stop, continue, scale) [thresholds to be calibrated, all values ILL]

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ad with ≥ 1,000 impressions and CTR < 0.6% on Meta feed/Reels | Replace creative |
| Landing sessions ≥ 100 per variant and LP→registration rate < 2% | Fix page (speed, form, offer clarity) before spending more |
| Cost per paid registration > 50% of expected value per registration for 3 consecutive days after learning | Pause ad set |
| Cost per registration ≤ target and attendance of the cohort is trending ≥ 50% | Continue |
| CPR ≤ 80% of target for 3 days, no drop in quality (attendance intent) | Scale budget +20% per 48h, never more than that |
| Data quality | Daily reconcile gateway payments vs platform conversions; stop if mismatch > 15% until fixed |
| Policy warnings/disapprovals | Pause, fix wording, never evade review |
| Day 13+ | Move budget to retargeting and urgency; no new cold audiences after Day 12 |

**Target CPR** should be derived, not guessed: allowable acquisition cost per registration = (₹999 net of tax and gateway fees) + (expected admission rate × contribution per admission × share attributable to ads) − (agreed profit buffer). Compute it in the model sheet (section 9).

## 7. Lead nurturing and conversion

General rules: consent per channel (WhatsApp opt-in, email opt-in, call consent), clear opt-out ("Reply STOP"), honour DNC/TRAI rules for calls, WhatsApp via approved templates for business-initiated messages, quiet hours 9pm-9am, **max frequency: WhatsApp ≤ 3 per week pre-event (≤ 1/day in the last 48h), email ≤ 3/week, calls ≤ 2 attempts per lead per phase**. Log every contact in the CRM.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Viewed offer, no registration | LP visit, no form | Retargeting ads D+1, D+3 (benefit + FAQ), D+7 (deadline); no WhatsApp/email unless a contact was given with consent | Ads | Return visit/registration |
| 2 | Started but didn't complete | Form/checkout abandoned | Within 1h: "Need help? Here's how payment works" (WA/email if consented), D+1 reminder with seats/deadline, D+2 counsellor call offer; stop after 3 touches | WA, email, optional call | Completion |

| 4 | Attendees, post-workshop | Check-in scan | Same day: thanks, slides, feedback form; D+1: assessment info/results date; invite to the community group; refund-claim instructions (as promised) | WA, email | Feedback, retention |

Refund handling: dedicated form, 48-hour decision SLA, refund within [7] working days, records kept; track refund rate as quality feedback.

## 11. Final recommendations

1. **Lock the terms first.** Clear rules, refund wording, tax treatment, and legal review are the highest-return tasks; ambiguous terms are the biggest reputational risk.
2. **Run one offer, one page, one topic** until data says otherwise; the 20-day window is too short for broad testing.
3. **Treat paid registrations as the leading metric and admissions as the goal.** Optimise for attendance (reminders, WhatsApp, clear logistics), because only attendees take the assessment and convert.
4. **Use the ₹999 fee as a commitment filter, not a profit centre.** Break-even on workshop cost is nice; admissions drive the return.
5. **Design for the person who doesn't win.** Handling the no-award group well produces future admissions, reviews, and referrals.
6. **Invest in proof you can verify**: real trainer bios, Karnal footage, student consent-based stories. Skip fake testimonials entirely.
7. **Keep the Karnal and Chandigarh identities truthful**: one workshop page, no fake address, a real Chandigarh page only when the centre exists.
8. **Respond fast:** reply to every inquiry within an hour during campaign hours; conversion falls sharply after that [A].
9. **Cap risk:** set a stop-loss (e.g., pause if CPR remains above 2× target after 5 days post-optimisation), reconcile payments daily, and keep the refund SLA.
10. **Verify the unverified.** Replace every [A], [T] and [ILL] with real data by Day 10: keyword volumes, competitor offers, CPCs, show rate, admission rate.
