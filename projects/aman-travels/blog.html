International Travel

7 things to check before your first international trip

  • Passport validity: most countries want at least 6 months remaining.
  • Visa rules for your destination, checked well in advance.
  • Travel insurance covering medical emergencies and cancellations.
  • Copies of tickets, hotel bookings and ID, on paper and on your phone.
  • Foreign currency or a forex card, plus a back-up payment option.
  • Airline baggage limits and cabin rules.
  • Vaccination or health requirements for your destination.

Destination Wedding

Destination wedding planning: where to start

  • Fix your guest count and budget first. Everything else follows.
  • Choose the season carefully: weather and peak-season prices both matter.
  • Block hotel rooms and plan guest travel early.
  • Plan transfers and a clear day-by-day schedule for guests.
  • Keep a local coordinator on call for the wedding days.
  • Get all costs in writing before you pay any advance.

Group Tours

How to plan a group tour without the chaos

  • Pick one person as the main decision maker and coordinator.
  • Agree on budget and travel dates before choosing a destination.
  • Book flights, trains and stays early for better group rates.
  • Keep the itinerary relaxed: fewer stops, more time to enjoy.
  • Share one document with all timings, contacts and bookings.
