# Business Model Canvas Strategy: Global Scholars' Hunt
Turning Point Studies Consultants, Karnal x eie European Business School
Date: 23 Oct 2026 | Campaign: 23 Oct to 11 Nov 2026 | Results live: 15 Nov 2026

**Goal:** 400 paid ₹999 registrations, then admissions. Client target: 300 admissions x ₹4 lakh = ₹12 crore (stretch). Realistic base: 12 to 32 admissions (base 20), assumptions only.

---

## 0. Launch se pehle: 4 cheezein jo strategy mein built-in hain

1. **Trust Pack (ads se pehle).** Jab tak ye likhit nahi aata, paid ad live nahi hoga aur "Dubai campus" kisi message mein nahi likha jayega:
   - KHDA/CAA licence aur UAE accreditation (agar Dubai entity hai)
   - Exact campus address
   - Degree kis body ke naam se milegi
   - eie ka likhit scholarship confirmation (301 scholarships, ₹4.5L, year 2 se 4 ka status)
   - Company registration, GST, office address, refund policy aur T&C ka public page

   Public sources eie ko Malta based (MFHEA regulated) dikhate hain, campus list mein Dubai nahi mila. Ye "campus nahi hai" ka proof nahi, sirf "confirm nahi hua" hai. Agar licence 26 Oct tak na aaye, to "eie European Business School pathway" likho, Dubai tabhi bolo jab kaagaz haath mein ho.
2. **Scholarship Ladder, merit contest nahi.** 301 scholarships aur lagbhag 400 entries ka matlab lagbhag 75% ko kuch milega. Copy mein "Scholarship Ladder", "chance", "T&C apply". "Guaranteed" ya "hunt jeeto" nahi. Selection method (draw, test ya ranking) T&C mein saaf likho.
3. **Parent ka Big Domino fraud aur hidden fees ka dar hai.** UVP parent ke liye trust aur clarity se shuru hoti hai, student ke liye scholarship hope se.
4. **Budget patla hai.** Non-paid channels core hain, add-on nahi.

## 1. Passion / Market
- Market: 12th ke baad abroad degree chahne wale North India ke families (Karnal, Panipat, Kurukshetra, Kaithal, Ambala, Yamunanagar, Delhi NCR suburbs).
- Primary core market: **Wealth.** Parent ke liye ye ₹15 se 35 lakh ka investment hai. Health (bacha safe hai?) aur Relationships secondary hain, objection handling mein use karo.

## 2. Niche
- **Kaun:** Haryana ki Karnal, Kurukshetra, Panipat, Kaithal belt ke middle aur upper-middle families. Bacha 12th pass hai, India mein achhi seat nahi mili ya bahut mehngi mili.
- Student: 18 se 22, 12th 50%+, phone-first. Buyer: parent, 38 se 55.
- **Buyer floor:** 50%+ (eligibility), scholarship ke baad ₹3 se 6 lakh/year jutane ki capacity (assumption), passport ready ya 2 se 3 mahine mein, parent abroad ke liye open.
- **Not ready:** 50% se kam, total budget ₹2 se 4 lakh, sirf "free" dhoondhne wale. Inhe ₹999 se pehle form par hi bata do, isse refund aur bad review kam honge.

## 3. Problem
**Big Domino:** "Parents ko samajh nahi aa raha ki bahar bhejna safe aur worth it hai ya nahi, aur kisi par bharosa nahi, kyunki fees, recognition aur fraud teeno ka dar hai."

Smaller problems:
1. 3 se 4 saal ka all-in cost kahin ek page par nahi.
2. Fake university aur commission wale consultant ka dar.
3. Degree India mein ya job ke liye maani jayegi ya nahi.
4. "₹999 le ke kuch nahi dete" ka shak, refund ka pata nahi.
5. India mein seat nahi mili, bacha ghar baith kar demotivate.
6. Student ka English aur akele rehne ka confidence.
7. Time pressure: result ke baad intake, aur "ek saal gap le lo" ka loop.

Real situations: vyapari ka beta 62% par; mummy ko WhatsApp scholarship forward ("pehle pata karo fraud to nahi"); do consultancies ke alag quotes; rishtedar ka bacha bahar gaya par job nahi mili; parent ka pehla sawal "refund milega?" aur "campus address dikhao".

## 4. UVP (Kano se)

**Must-Haves:** free first counselling (24 ghante ke andar), documents aur visa guidance, scholarship aur loan help, **Trust Pack**, local reviews aur past admits (abhi data nahi), public T&C aur refund page.

**Performance Benefits:** consultancy fee aur ₹30,000 waiver ka clear math; ek page par 3 se 4 saal ki personal Cost Sheet; counselling call 24 ghante mein, ek hi counsellor ka naam; Kurukshetra, Panipat, Kaithal town pages aur Hinglish content.

**Delighters (kisi competitor ke paas nahi mile):**
1. Rupee-value Scholarship Ladder, 12th pass 50%+ ke liye (competitors ke waivers 80 se 90%+ marks par tike hain).
2. ₹30,000 consultancy fee waiver ladder ke 300 students ko.
3. Live public results, 15 Nov, Facebook/Instagram.
4. Karnal Sector 8 office par parent seminar aur kaagaz dekhna.
5. Free French course bonus (provider aur hours confirm hone ke baad hi claim).

**Unique Mechanism: "Proof-First Pathway".** Parent ko paisa dene se pehle teen kaagaz milte hain: eie ka licence aur degree proof, 3 se 4 saal ki cost sheet, aur scholarship letter. Order badalta hai: pehle saboot, phir paisa.

**Guarantee: "Sach-Pehle Refund Guarantee"** (conditional). Agar counselling call attend ki, 7 din mein documents diye, parent meeting attend ki, aur phir bhi likhit mein teeno kaagaz (licence aur degree proof, cost sheet, ladder jeeta to scholarship letter) nahi mile, to ₹999 poora refund, 7 working days mein, bina sawal ke. Saath mein: scholarship letter haath mein aane se pehle koi admission fee ya deposit nahi maangi jayegi. Ye scholarship jeetne ki guarantee nahi hai. Refund terms client se confirm karne hain.

## 5. MVP

**Island #1 (abhi):** parent darra aur confused, bacha weak ya mehngi seat ke beech atka, koi proof ya total cost nahi pata.
**Island #2 (desired):** family ke haath mein verified proof, cost sheet aur scholarship letter, Karnal office mein baith kar sab dekha, aur 2027 intake ke liye clear haan ya na.

| # | Milestone | Kya hota hai | Purana tareeka jo replace hota hai |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Verify (Day 0 se 1)** | ₹999 receipt aur T&C email, Trust Pack WhatsApp par, counselling call 24 ghante mein | Anjaan agent aur WhatsApp forward par bharosa |
| 2 | **See the Numbers (Day 2 se 14)** | Parent meeting (office ya Zoom), personal Cost Sheet, eligibility aur buyer floor check | "Free" ke naam par hidden charges |
| 3 | **Results Day (15 Nov)** | Live result, personal WhatsApp result, scholarship letter 48 ghante mein (eie se confirm). ₹35k winners ke liye pehle se likhi follow-up call | Result ka pata hi nahi chalta |
| 4 | **Decide and Lock (16 Nov se 15 Dec)** | Application, deposit, loan help, visa checklist, French course start | "Pehle result lao, ek saal gap lo" ka loop |

**Front-end offer:** ₹999 Global Scholars' Hunt entry. **Back-end:** Admission plus end-to-end support (application, visa, loan, accommodation, pre-departure), phir PG pathway aur referrals.

## 6. Market Message
**Template:** "Karnal aur nazdeeki shehron ke 12th pass students aur parents ke liye: fraud, hidden fees aur 'kahan bheju' ke confusion se nikaal kar, 23 din mein verified eie European Business School pathway aur scholarship letter, **Proof-First Pathway** ke saath." (Dubai ka naam tabhi jodo jab Trust Pack confirm ho.)

**Variation 1, Student:** "12th mein 50% par bhi abroad degree ka chance. Global Scholars' Hunt: ₹4.5 lakh tak ki scholarship ladder, ₹999 mein. Result 15 Nov, live. T&C apply."

**Variation 2, Parent:** "Pehle licence, address aur total kharcha dekho, phir decide karo. Turning Point, Karnal: ₹999 mein counselling, cost sheet aur refund policy likhit mein. Fraud ka dar? Office aaiye, kaagaz dekhiye."

## 7. Channel
Budget ₹2 lakh: Meta ₹1L, Google ₹50k, influencers ₹50k (plan jaisa). Sirf paid se ₹500 per registration nahi banega, isliye non-paid core hai.

**Primary: local trust channels**
- **School aur coaching tie-ups** (Karnal, Panipat, Kurukshetra, Kaithal): teachers aur coaching owners ko referral partner banao, 20 minute ka parent talk, QR code. Sabse kam cost, sabse zyada trust.
- **Referrals:** purane parents, "ek family laao" reward (client confirm kare).
- **Offline:** Karnal office par Saturday parent seminars (20 se 25 families per batch), Diwali se pehle slots.
- **WhatsApp:** counsellor ka personal follow-up (WhatsApp Business templates).
- **Local micro-influencers:** script mein Trust Pack ho, hype nahi.

**Secondary: paid**
- **Meta:** instant form ya landing page, do creatives (student: ladder; parent: proof, address, refund), retargeting 3 se 7 din.
- **Google Search:** "study in dubai after 12th", "12th ke baad abroad", town names. Competitors ke "Dubai x Karnal" pages nahi hain.
- **Organic aur SEO:** Google Business Profile reviews, Hinglish town pages, refund aur T&C page, "degree India mein valid hai?" explainer.

Calendar: Diwali 2026 lagbhag 8 Nov (date confirm karo). 9 se 11 Nov last-call push.

## 8. Price
**Front-end ₹999.** Parent ke liye "trap ya test?" ka sawal hai, isliye price ke saath refund aur proof zaroori hai.

**₹999 mein cover hona chahiye (recommendation, client confirm kare):** ladder entry, 20 minute 1:1 counselling aur parent meeting, personal Cost Sheet, Trust Pack access, French course (jab provider confirm ho), live aur personal result, Sach-Pehle Refund Guarantee. Recommendation: ₹999 admit hone par consultancy fee mein adjust ho jaye.

**Anchor (sirf real list prices par, nahi to mat chalao):** "~~₹17,000~~ ₹999". Counselling aur cost sheet ~₹5,000, French course ~₹12,000 (dono assumption, client se real list value lo).

**Value ladder**
| Step | Offer | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hunt entry | ₹999 |
| 2 | Parent seminar aur closing meeting | ₹999 ke andar |
| 3 | Admission plus support | Consultancy fee list ₹60,000 (assumption), ladder winners ₹30,000 after waiver |
| 4 | Seat deposit | ₹50,000 recommended (confirm, refund terms ke saath) |
| 5 | eie tuition (university ko seedha pay) | Year-1 ~₹4.5L list, scholarship ke baad ~₹3.95L average (assumption) |
| 6 | PG pathway, referrals | TBD |

Parent tuition seedha university ko pay kare, agent ko nahi. Ye trust point hai.

## 9. Delivery
- **Attention:** 1:1 counselling (20 min), parent seminar (20 se 25 families), closing 1:1 Karnal office par.
- **DWY vs DFY:** documents aur application DFY. Cost decision parent khud sheet dekh kar le (DWY).
- **Channels:** WhatsApp Business (primary), Zoom, call, email.
- **Response time:** counselling call 24 ghante, WhatsApp reply 2 ghante (working hours), refund aur complaint 48 ghante.
- **Community:** WhatsApp community "Global Scholars 2027", weekly deadline reminders, parent-to-parent introductions (consent ke saath).
- **Team:** 2 counsellors, 1 admin, 1 content person. 400 calls x 20 min = lagbhag 133 ghante, 3 hafton mein manageable.
- **Data:** DPDP Act consent, minors ke liye parent consent.
